    Foundations

    Color, type, space and shape.

    All lengths are iOS points at 1x on a 393 × 852 canvas. Token names match the asset catalog and the Figma variables.

    Color

    Each swatch shows its light and dark value. Three hues carry meaning; the rest are surfaces and ink.

    Approved pairs

    Text and background combinations that pass contrast. Ratios are for the light theme. Use only these.

    Typography

    Plus Jakarta Sans, scaled like the iOS text styles so Dynamic Type works everywhere.

    Spacing

    A 4pt grid. The screen margin is space-4, 16pt.

    Sizes

    Fixed dimensions for controls and bars.

    Radius

    Five radii and a capsule, drawn with continuous corners on device.

    Motion

    System springs, nothing custom.

    Four durations and three curves that approximate SwiftUI's named springs. Under Reduce Motion every movement becomes a crossfade.

    Sheet presentation

    The sheet slides up while the background dims. The system animates it.

    duration-slow 400mseasing-smoothscrim 250ms

    Balance count-up

    After a cash out the balance rolls to its new value on monospaced digits.

    The only bouncy moment in the app. It marks money on the way.
