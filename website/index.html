    iOS · Version 1

    Points, not pixels. Green for money, navy for waiting.

    One system for the cash-out flow on iPhone: a single type family, five radii, three colors with a job each, and components that mirror their SwiftUI initializers.

      The rules

      Eight decisions that replaced the inconsistencies in the original Figma file.

      Foundations

      Color, type, space and shape.

      All lengths are iOS points at 1x on a 393 × 852 canvas. Token names match the asset catalog and the Figma variables.

      Color

      Each swatch shows its light and dark value. Three hues carry meaning; the rest are surfaces and ink.

      Approved pairs

      Text and background combinations that pass contrast. Ratios are for the light theme. Use only these.

      Typography

      Plus Jakarta Sans, scaled like the iOS text styles so Dynamic Type works everywhere.

      Spacing

      A 4pt grid. The screen margin is space-4, 16pt.

      Sizes

      Fixed dimensions for controls and bars.

      Radius

      Five radii and a capsule, drawn with continuous corners on device.

      Components

      Ten views, built once.

      Each example below is the live component. Props mirror the Swift initializers, and the snippet is ready to paste.

      Screens

      One sheet, every state.

      The cash-out screen is a system sheet. Its states share a layout, so the hero color and the primary action are the only things that change.

      Cash out sheet

      Presented with the system sheet, drag indicator and scrim. Never draw the chrome into the screen.

      Figma: CashoutSheet · State=Earned

      Prototype

      Tap through the whole flow.

      Home, the cash-out sheet, History, cashing out, success and the wait timer. Tap the green Cash out button on the phone to start, or pick a starting state beside it.

      Motion

      System springs, nothing custom.

      Four durations and three curves that approximate SwiftUI's named springs. Under Reduce Motion every movement becomes a crossfade.

      Sheet presentation

      The sheet slides up while the background dims. The system animates it.

      duration-slow 400mseasing-smoothscrim 250ms

      Balance count-up

      After a cash out the balance rolls to its new value on monospaced digits.

      duration-count 600mseasing-snappy.numericText()

      Success pop

      The only bouncy moment in the app. It marks money on the way.

      duration-slow 400mseasing-bouncy

      Press and tick

      Controls dip on press. Countdown digits roll as they change.

      duration-fast 150msduration-base 250mseasing-snappy

      Tokens

      Web equivalents of the SwiftUI springs. In the app, prefer the named springs.
